How it works

Add any tool with a read-only token — two minutes each. Stores, ad accounts, inboxes, projects. Nestral only ever reads.

The morning brief

Signal.
Not another dashboard.

Dashboards make you go looking. Nestral comes to you — and only when something deserves attention. Connect what you run; here's the kind of thing it surfaces and ranks into one list:

  • 📉
    Momentum shifts

    Revenue or activity down vs the prior week, anywhere.

  • 🚫
    Gone quiet

    Something with no activity in days — is it live, is it working?

  • 📦
    Backlogs

    Orders, tickets, or tasks piling up unhandled.

  • 🪫
    Risk thresholds

    Low stock, low balance, anything crossing a line you set.

  • 💳
    Money to move

    Pending payments, captures, or approvals waiting on you.

  • 🔌
    Broken connections

    Expired tokens, before they cost you a day of data.

FAQ

The short version.

Who's it for?

Anyone running more than their attention can hold — multi-brand commerce operators, agencies watching client accounts, solo founders with many moving parts, people juggling side-projects. If you check more than one dashboard a day, it's for you.

What can it connect to?

Shopify at launch (orders, inventory, fulfillment). Meta & TikTok ads, Stripe, Klaviyo, then inboxes and project tools are on the near-term roadmap. The model is simple: connect a source, and it joins your brief.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Read-only API scopes, your own tokens, talking directly to each platform. Never sold, never used to train models. Disconnect anything instantly.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Connecting a source is copy-pasting one token. After that, you just read the brief — by Telegram, email, or the cockpit.

When does it launch?
